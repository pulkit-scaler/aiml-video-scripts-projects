# Support

## Support hours
Support for the Team plan is available by email, Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 18:00 in the customer's region. Enterprise customers also get 24/7 phone support.

## Response times
Enterprise priority 1 incidents, where the service is down or a whole team is blocked, get a first response within 1 hour, at any time of day. Priority 2 issues get a first response within 4 business hours. Team plan tickets get a first response within 1 business day.
