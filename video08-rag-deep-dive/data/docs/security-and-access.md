# Security and access

## Single sign-on
Single sign-on (SSO) with Okta, Azure AD and Google Workspace is available on the Enterprise plan only. Team plan workspaces sign in with email and password, with optional two-factor authentication.

## Account lockout
After 5 failed sign-in attempts, an account is locked for 15 minutes. A workspace admin can unlock an account immediately from the Members page. If every admin of a workspace is locked out, support can restore access after verifying the account owner.

## Two-factor authentication
Workspace admins can require two-factor authentication for every member. Members without it are asked to set it up at their next sign-in.

## Encryption
All customer data is encrypted in transit with TLS 1.2 or higher and at rest with AES-256.
