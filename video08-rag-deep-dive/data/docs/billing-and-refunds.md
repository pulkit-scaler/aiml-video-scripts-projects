# Billing and refunds

## Monthly plans
Monthly plans are billed automatically on the same day every calendar month. A customer on a monthly plan can cancel at any time from the Billing page, and the plan stays active until the end of the paid month. Refunds for monthly plans are only available within 7 days of a charge, and only if the workspace has not been used since that charge.

## Annual plans
Annual plans are paid up front for twelve months and include a 15% discount compared with paying monthly. A customer on an annual plan can request a full refund within 30 days of purchase. After 30 days, annual plans are not refundable, but the customer can turn auto-renew off so the plan simply ends at the term date.

## Adding seats
Seats added in the middle of a billing cycle are pro-rated: the customer pays only for the days left in the current cycle. Removing seats takes effect at the next renewal, and removed seats are never refunded.

## Plans bought through a reseller
If a customer bought Northwind Cloud through a reseller or marketplace, the reseller owns the billing relationship. Refund requests for these plans must go to the reseller, not to Northwind Cloud support.

## Failed payments
If a card payment fails, we retry three times over 10 days and email the billing contact each time. After the third failed retry, the workspace becomes read-only until the invoice is paid.
