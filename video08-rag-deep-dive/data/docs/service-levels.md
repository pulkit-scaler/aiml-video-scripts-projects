# Service levels

## Status page and uptime history
Northwind Cloud publishes live status and uptime history at status.northwind.cloud. Every incident is posted there with a start time, an end time and a short summary. Customers can subscribe to uptime alerts by email or Slack, and the uptime history for the last 12 months is always visible on the page.

## Team plan uptime
The Team plan has an uptime target of 99.5% per calendar month. The target is a goal, not a contractual guarantee, and Team plan customers do not receive service credits when uptime falls below it.

## Enterprise SLA
Enterprise customers get a contractual commitment of 99.95% monthly availability, measured by our external monitors. If availability in a month falls below 99.95%, the customer receives a service credit of 10% of that month's fee, rising to 25% below 99.0%. Credits must be claimed within 30 days of the end of the affected month.

## Planned maintenance
Planned maintenance is announced at least 72 hours in advance and is scheduled on Sundays between 02:00 and 04:00 UTC. Planned maintenance does not count against availability.
