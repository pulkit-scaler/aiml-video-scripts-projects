# Sources for facts quoted in the scripts

Every outside fact in these three scripts, where it's used, and where to check it.

| Fact in the script | Video | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Air Canada's chatbot gave a customer wrong bereavement-fare rules. A tribunal held the airline responsible: the chatbot "is still just a part of Air Canada's website". Total award C$812.02 (C$650.88 damages, C$36.14 interest, C$125 fees). | 1 | [Moffatt v. Air Canada, 2024 BCCRT 149](https://decisions.civilresolutionbc.ca/crt/crtd/en/525448/1/document.do), 14 Feb 2024. Also reported by [Daily Hive](https://dailyhive.com/canada/air-canada-chatbot-decision). Cited in Chip Huyen, *AI Engineering* (O'Reilly), Ch. 3. |
| 95% accuracy per step falls to about 60% over 10 steps, and to 0.6% over 100 steps. | 10 | *AI Engineering*, Ch. 6, "Agent Overview". The arithmetic checks out: 0.95 to the power 10 is 0.599, and to the power 100 is 0.006. It assumes each step can fail independently and nothing retries. |
| ML knowledge is "no longer a must-have" for building AI applications, but "still extremely valuable" because it helps troubleshooting. | 10 | *AI Engineering*, Ch. 1, "AI Engineering Versus ML Engineering". Always quote both halves. |
